Parking Space Calibration Tool

Draw polygons directly on the live camera feed to define parking spaces

📋 How to Calibrate:

  • Click on the video feed to mark the corners of a parking space (4 clicks to complete)
  • The polygon will automatically close after the 4th point
  • Each completed space is saved to the list on the right
  • Click on a saved space to edit or delete it
  • Use different colors to organize spaces by zones or types
  • Press "Save All" when finished to apply calibration to the system
